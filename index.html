Where your report goes

Your PDF stays in this browser and is never uploaded. When you ask a question, only the passages retrieved for that question are sent to the model provider to draft an answer.

Nothing is stored after you clear the report or close the tab.

  1. Read. Text is extracted page by page in your browser, keeping printed page numbers.
  2. Index. Passages are indexed for keyword and meaning search, also in your browser.
  3. Ask. The answer cites passages; quotes and figures are checked against them before anything is shown as supported.
